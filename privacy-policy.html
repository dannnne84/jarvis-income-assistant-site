Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This app ("Jarvis Income Assistant") is a personal automation tool used to generate and publish short-form video content to a single TikTok account that the app owner also owns and operates. It is not a public service and does not offer accounts, sign-ups, or functionality to any third party.

What data we access

Through TikTok's Content Posting API, this app accesses only what is required to upload and publish video content on the authorized account: the account's access token and open ID, provided directly by TikTok after the account owner authorizes the app. We do not access followers, direct messages, or any other TikTok user's data.

What data we store

The TikTok access token and refresh token are stored locally on the account owner's own machine, in a local configuration file that is never transmitted to any third party or uploaded anywhere. Video content, scripts, and voiceovers generated by this app are also stored locally.

What data we share

We do not sell, share, or transmit any data to third parties. Video content is sent only to TikTok, for the sole purpose of publishing it on the authorized account.

Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as needed to operate the app and can be deleted at any time by removing the local configuration file and revoking the app's authorization from within TikTok's own settings.

Contact

This is a single-operator personal project. Questions can be directed to the account owner via the contact method associated with the TikTok developer account under which this app is registered.
